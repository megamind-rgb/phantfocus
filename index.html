A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you begin.

When does it launch?+

In November 2026. Join the launch list and we’ll send you one email when PHANTFOCUS is ready, with your 80% off.

How does the 80% off work?+

Join the launch list before launch and you get 80% off PHANTFOCUS for life: on your first purchase and, if it’s sold as a subscription, on every renewal for as long as you keep it. At launch we email you a personal link or code, one per email address. You’ll see the price before you buy, and you can still decide not to. The details are in the terms.

What happens when I lock in?+

Your chosen apps and websites stay available. Other apps that come to the front are blocked or hidden, depending on your blocking mode. Sessions don’t pause; if you need to end one early, hold Exit for three seconds.

Blocking needs the Accessibility and Automation access you allow at setup. Websites are checked in Safari, Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Chromium and Arc. Other browsers, such as Firefox, are blocked during a lock-in.

Does my focus history leave my Mac?+

No. PHANTFOCUS keeps your focus history and settings on your Mac, and needs no account. It reads which app and browser tab are in front only during a lock-in. It goes online only for updates: every 6 hours it asks GitHub whether there’s a new version. You can turn that off.

The launch list is separate: if you join, we keep your email address only to send you the launch email with your discount. You can unsubscribe any time, and we delete the list at the latest 6 months after that email. Privacy has the details.

Will it work on my Mac?+

PHANTFOCUS runs on macOS 13 Ventura or newer, on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs. A notch is not required to use the app.

I already have the free preview.+

Keep using it: from version 0.2.0 on, it updates itself, so there’s nothing to download. If yours is older, email us and we’ll help you move to the newest version.

Does the website demo block my apps?+

No. The preview here only demonstrates the timer and dashboard in your browser, with sample activity. The Mac app does the real blocking and tracks your own sessions.
