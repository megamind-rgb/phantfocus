Good to know

Before
you settle in.

Is this a separate app from PHANTFOCUS?+

lattefocus is the soft, rose-toned look inside PHANTFOCUS. The download contains one app with both looks. Once it’s open, go to Settings → Look → lattefocus. You can switch back whenever you like. Prefer black and chrome? See PHANTFOCUS: it’s the same download.

Will it work on my Mac?+

PHANTFOCUS is a universal app for both Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. It’s a Mac app; the preview on this page is available in any modern browser.

How do I install and open it?+

Download PHANTFOCUS. Safari unzips it for you; in other browsers, double-click PHANTFOCUS.zip. Drag PHANTFOCUS.app to Applications, then open it.

The first time, macOS says it can’t verify PHANTFOCUS, because it isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Close that message, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway and enter your password. On macOS 13 and 14 you can also Control-click the app and choose Open, then Open.

When PHANTFOCUS asks, allow Accessibility: it keeps the Dock and other apps away during a focus session. At your first focus session, macOS also asks to let PHANTFOCUS control System Events and your browser. Allow both: that’s how it sees which app and tab are in front and blocks anything not on your list. Clicked Don’t Allow? Turn PHANTFOCUS on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility or Automation.

Then choose Settings → Look → lattefocus.

How do I update?+

PHANTFOCUS looks for new versions by itself. When there’s one, an Update button appears at the top of the dashboard: click it, then Install and Relaunch. It downloads and checks the new version, then reopens. Your history and settings stay. You can also choose PHANTFOCUS → Check for Updates… at any time.

During a focus session the update waits: click Update again when it’s over. If PHANTFOCUS can’t replace itself, for example when it runs straight from Downloads or your Mac account isn’t an administrator, it tells you. Then quit it, download the new version from this page and replace the copy in Applications.

Have version 0.1.0? That one can’t update itself yet: quit it, download PHANTFOCUS once more from this page, replace the copy in Applications and open it like the first time. Your history stays. After that, it updates itself.

Does my focus history leave my Mac?+

The app stores your focus history and activity locally on your Mac. No account is needed. This website’s demo is separate: it uses illustrative activity and runs a timer in your browser only.

Can I change the session length and appearance?+

Choose a 30, 45, or 90-minute session, or set your own duration from 5 minutes to 6 hours. The moon and sun button switches lattefocus between its light and dark appearances. Try both in the preview above.
